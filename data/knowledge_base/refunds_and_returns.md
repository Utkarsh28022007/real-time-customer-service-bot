# Refunds and Return Policy

## 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee
- We accept returns within **30 days** of delivery for most items in new, unused condition with original packaging, tags, and accessories intact.
- Non-returnable items include: digital downloads, customized/engraved merchandise, perishable items, and gift cards.

## How to Initiate a Return
1. Log into your account portal or request a return authorization through this customer chat assistant.
2. Provide your original Order ID and select the item(s) you wish to return with a brief reason.
3. A prepaid shipping return label will be emailed to you immediately.
4. Affix the prepaid label to your box and drop it off at any authorized UPS/FedEx drop-off location.

## Return Shipping Fees & Restocking
- Returns due to manufacturer defects, wrong items sent, or damage in transit are **100% free** with prepaid return shipping covered by us.
- Elective returns (buyer's remorse, wrong size ordered) incur a standard $5.99 return shipping deduction from the refund total.
- We do NOT charge any restocking fees on standard consumer products.

## Refund Processing Timeline
- Once our warehouse receives and inspects the return (typically 2-4 business days), your refund is approved.
- Refunds are credited back to the original payment method:
  - Credit/Debit cards: 3-5 business days depending on your financial institution.
  - PayPal / Digital Wallets: 24-48 hours.
  - Store credit: Instant.
- You will receive an email confirmation as soon as your refund has been issued.
