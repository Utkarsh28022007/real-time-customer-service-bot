# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Shipping Methods and Delivery Times
- **Standard Shipping**: 3-5 business days within the continental US. Free on orders over $50; otherwise, a flat rate of $4.99 applies.
- **Expedited Shipping**: 2 business days. Flat rate of $12.99.
- **Overnight Express**: Next business day delivery for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST. Flat rate of $24.99.
- **International Shipping**: 7-14 business days depending on customs clearance. Rates calculated dynamically at checkout based on destination country and weight.

## Order Processing
- Orders placed Monday through Friday before 2:00 PM EST begin processing the same business day.
- Orders placed on weekends or federal holidays are processed on the next business day.
- Once dispatched, customers receive an automated confirmation email containing a carrier tracking number (e.g., FedEx, UPS, or DHL).

## Tracking Your Package
- Track your parcel anytime using our online order portal or by providing your Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXX`) to our automated customer service assistant.
- Tracking status definitions:
  - **Processing**: Order is being prepared and packed in our fulfillment center.
  - **In Transit**: Handed off to carrier and currently on route to destination.
  - **Out for Delivery**: The carrier has scheduled delivery for today.
  - **Delivered**: Parcel has been placed at the recipient address.

## Lost or Damaged Packages
- If tracking indicates "Delivered" but you have not received your shipment, please check porches, side doors, and with neighbors. Allow up to 24 hours before reporting.
- To file a claim for lost or damaged goods, contact support within 7 calendar days of delivery with your Order ID and photo evidence for damaged items.
