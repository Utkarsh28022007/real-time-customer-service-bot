# Billing, Payments, and Subscriptions

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit and Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover.
- Digital Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Shop Pay.
- Financing: Klarna and Afterpay (available on qualifying orders above $50).

## Subscription Plans & Recurring Charges
- **Basic Plan**: $9.99/month, billed monthly. Cancel anytime with no penalty.
- **Pro Plan**: $29.99/month or $299/year (saving 17%). Includes priority support and advanced analytics.
- **Enterprise Plan**: Custom tier with dedicated account managers and SLAs.
- Subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled at least 24 hours prior to renewal date.

## Updating Billing Information
- You can update your payment method, view invoice history, and download PDF receipts in the Account Settings > Billing section.
- If a renewal payment fails, our system retries every 48 hours up to 3 times before pausing access.

## Disputes and Unauthorized Charges
- If you notice an unrecognized charge on your bank statement, please contact our support team immediately before initiating a bank chargeback so we can issue a prompt resolution or refund.
